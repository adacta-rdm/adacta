The Catalog contains manufacturer and product definitions. A product describes a model of equipment. An inventory entry describes one physical item in the laboratory. {% .lead %}

## Browse and search products

Select **Catalog** in the sidebar. The catalog lists products with their manufacturer, product number, description, and photograph when available.

Use the search field to narrow the list. Each word must appear in the product number, product name, description, manufacturer name, or series name. For example, a manufacturer name and part of a product number can identify one model.

Select a manufacturer to see its products. Products in a series are shown together. Specifications shared by every member appear above the table. Specifications that differ appear in the table. Products without a series appear separately.

Select a product to see its specifications, measurement channels, and source information.

## Logos and photographs

A manufacturer can have a logo. A product can have a photograph. Products in the same series can share one photograph. A manufacturer without a logo shows the first letter of its name.

Catalog images are stored as original files. The application keeps their original bytes and records who added them and when.

Catalog images currently enter the application with the seed preset. The catalog pages do not provide image upload or editing controls. The demo preset includes a logo and a photograph of a fictional flow controller.
