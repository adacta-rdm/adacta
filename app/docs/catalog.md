The Catalog contains manufacturer and product definitions. A product describes a model of equipment. An inventory entry describes one physical item in the laboratory. {% .lead %}

## Browse and search products

Select **Catalog** in the sidebar. The catalog lists products with their manufacturer, product number, description, and photograph when available.

Use the search field to narrow the list. Each word must appear in the product number, product name, description, manufacturer name, or series name. For example, a manufacturer name and part of a product number can identify one model.

Select a manufacturer to see its products. Products in a series are shown together. Specifications shared by every member appear above the table. Specifications that differ appear in the table. Products without a series appear separately.

Select a product to see its specifications, measurement channels, and source information.

## Import physical equipment

Select the products that represent physical units in the laboratory, then choose **Import selected**. The selection remains while you search or open a manufacturer. The form creates one equipment entry for each selected product.

Equipment takes its name from the linked product. A serial number is optional. A recorded serial number identifies one unit of a product, including archived equipment. Different products can share a serial number. Some models request unit-specific values, such as a calibrated range. Supply every required value before importing.

Every equipment unit refers to a product. A product with equipment is archived when it is no longer used; it is retained so its units keep their model information. Model-wide specifications and measurement channels remain on the product. Values that distinguish one physical unit are recorded on its equipment entry.

## Add equipment manually

Select **Add inventory item** in Inventory and choose **Equipment** when the Catalog does not contain the model. Search the Catalog first to reduce duplicate product definitions. The form creates one physical unit and one reusable product marked **User entered**.

Choose an existing manufacturer when possible. Otherwise select **Add a manufacturer** and enter its name. Use **Unknown manufacturer** when the maker cannot be identified.

Product name is required. Model number, description, and serial number are optional. The form does not collect specifications or measurement channels.

Use the manufacturer's model designation for the product. Serial numbers and unit-specific specifications distinguish physical units of the same product.

## Logos and photographs

A manufacturer can have a logo. A product can have a photograph. Products in the same series can share one photograph. A manufacturer without a logo shows the first letter of its name.

Catalog images are stored as original files. The application keeps their original bytes and records who added them and when.

Catalog images currently enter the application with the seed preset. The catalog pages do not provide image upload or editing controls. The demo preset includes a logo and a photograph of a fictional flow controller.
